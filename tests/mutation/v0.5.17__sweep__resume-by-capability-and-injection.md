## What the sweep did not cover, and the one thing it could not

- `inject.ps1` is not Python and is not swept. Its one defect this release -- the function-scope accumulator that returned an empty window list -- was caught by the **live wired run**, not by any test or mutant; that is recorded in the v0.5.17 checklist's results and in the driver's own comment.
- The consent prompt's console branch and `_ask_inject_on_console`'s `input()` path are exercised only by the no-console test.

**A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.**
