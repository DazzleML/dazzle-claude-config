## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Not attempted by the generator: the CLI wiring (`cli.py` box load, the `not for` block), which the checklist covers by eye, and the two call sites in `apply.py`/`collect.py` (covered by the direction tests, which fail under M9).
