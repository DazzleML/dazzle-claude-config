## Anomalies (read first)

**The first run reported 10 of 12 mutants as `apply-failed` ("found 0").** Every failure was a multi-line `original`; the two single-line mutants applied. Cause: the working tree is CRLF (`core.autocrlf=true`) and the harness searched the decoded bytes for the LF-only spec text. Any multi-line mutant was unapplicable on this checkout, and the prior v0.4.0 sweep never noticed because all twelve of its originals were single-line. Fixed in the harness (`apply_mutation` matches and writes in the file's own EOL convention, so the restore byte-check still holds), with a regression test that fails on the old harness and passes on the new (`test_crlf_file_matches_lf_spec_multiline`; harness suite 17 -> 18). The sweep below is the re-run. Restore verified `ok` for every mutant in every round; production files byte-identical to HEAD afterwards.

## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** The generator saw docstrings that state intent, which is why its mutants attacked rules rather than syntax; on the direction block and the verbs' skip handling it produced one mutant each, so those surfaces are thinly sampled here.
