## Caveats

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing -- and **a clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Not attempted by the generator: the CLI table renderer (`_run_base`, `_loss_row`), `read_base_from`'s spec parsing beyond what the tests already pin, and `_write_back`'s `live_only` branch (pinned by `test_accept_on_a_supplied_base_writes_live_only`).
